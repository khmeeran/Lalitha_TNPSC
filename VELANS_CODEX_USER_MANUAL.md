## 📱 Chapter I: Inscribing the App on Your Device (PWA Installation)

You do not need to open a boring browser tab every morning or type confusing web addresses. Inscribe the **TNPSC Wizard** directly onto your home screen so you can tap and conquer anywhere—even offline!

### 🤖 For Android Wizards (Google Chrome)
1. **Open the Realm URL** in Google Chrome: `http://localhost:3000` (or the deployed Vercel link).
2. **Watch for the Golden Scroll Banner**: Look at the bottom of your screen. A golden parchment will whisper:  
   *"Inscribe TNPSC Wizard onto your device for offline trials and native speed!"*
3. **Tap "Inscribe Now"** ⚡ (or tap the **three dots ⋮** in the top-right corner of Chrome).
4. Select **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home Screen"** 📲.
5. Confirm when prompted. A royal purple and gold wizard crest will materialize on your home screen!
6. **Launch like a native game**: Tap the icon to play full-screen with zero browser address bars!

### 🍏 For Apple iOS Wizards (Safari on iPhone / iPad)
1. **Open the Realm URL** inside Apple Safari.
2. Look at the bottom bar of Safari and tap the **Share Button** ⎋ (the square icon with an upward arrow).
3. Scroll down the menu options until you find **"Add to Home Screen"** ➕.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner.
5. Behold! The **TNPSC Wizard** app icon is permanently docked on your iPhone screen. Tap it to enter the sanctuary with true standalone immersion!
